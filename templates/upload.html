Viva Demonstration Guide:

During external viva examination, you can upload any leaf or fruit photograph (e.g., diseased mango with black anthracnose lesions, orange with citrus canker, or an apple leaf).

1. The image is resized to (224, 224, 3) and normalized for neural processing.
2. The model extracts feature probabilities.
3. RDFLib queries the Protégé OWL ontology to retrieve the causal pathogen, clinical symptoms, and multi-category treatment protocols.
4. A structured diagnostic report is generated, complete with download options.
