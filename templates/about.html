🌱 About Our Project

Fruit Disease Detection System

The Fruit Disease Detection System is a web application that uses Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to detect diseases in fruits. It helps farmers and users identify infected fruits early and provides useful suggestions to prevent the spread of disease and improve crop health.
