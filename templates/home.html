Dual-Engine AI Architecture

Unlike standard black-box classifiers, our system marries Machine Learning (which answers "What visual disease pattern is present in this image?") with OWL/SWRL Semantic Ontology (which answers "What pathogen causes it, what are the clinical symptoms, what is its severity, and how do we safely remediate it?").

How The System Works

Step-by-step diagnostic reasoning pipeline from raw image capture to actionable agrarian treatment recommendations.

1
Upload Image

Farmer uploads leaf or fruit specimen image.

2
Image Processing

Validation, resize to 224x224, and tensor normalization.

3
Disease Prediction

CNN inference classifies candidate pathology.

4
Ontology Reasoning

SPARQL queries Protégé OWL axioms & SWRL rules.

5
Final Diagnosis

Synthesizes cause, symptoms, and severity ranking.

6
Care & Prevention

Delivers safe organic, biological & cultural remedies.

Capabilities

System Features & Modules

AI Disease Detection

Automated deep learning classification capable of detecting complex pathologies across multiple fruit species with confidence metrics.

Image Analysis

Multi-stage computer vision preprocessing pipeline incorporating lanczos resizing, z-score normalization, and data augmentation.

Ontology Reasoning

Protégé-compatible OWL 2 ontology parsed via RDFLib with SPARQL semantic queries and SWRL rule inference for explainable diagnoses.

Disease Information

Comprehensive phytopathology directory detailing pathogens, environmental factors, visual symptoms, and host fruit vulnerabilities.

Care Suggestions

Categorized guidance spanning Organic, Biological, Cultural, and responsible Agricultural Chemical protectants.

Prevention Guidance

Proactive field sanitation, micro-drip irrigation practices, canopy management, and resistant cultivar selection advice.
